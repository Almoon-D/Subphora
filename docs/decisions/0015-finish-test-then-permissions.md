# 0015 — Finish the project, test on the owner's land, then seek permissions

- Date: 2026-11-24 · Status: accepted · Owner ruling · Refines 0014 · MASTER_PLAN §1
- Ruling: the whole build path (sessions 02–16) comes first. At the end the owner tests
  Subphora on his own land, without selling anything. Only if it works and satisfies him
  does he contact the organisations whose terms a paid product would need.
- Consequence: the commercial questions of 0014 are not decided now and do not block any
  session; the design stays single-owner (§4.9) until a later decision. Sessions keep using
  each service within its current free terms.
- Tracking: `docs/commercial-use.md` lists every organisation to contact, with the term,
  its source and what to ask. Any session that finds a term a paid product would need adds
  a row there (AGENTS.md → Session workflow).
