# 0023 — Regional detail first, the national service as baseline and fallback

- Date: 2026-11-24 · Status: accepted · Owner ruling · Amends 0022 · MASTER_PLAN §3, §4.1
- Ruling: where a region, province or basin authority publishes a more detailed official
  service for a theme, the map uses it there; the national service (MITECO, IGME, SoilGrids)
  stays the baseline everywhere and the fallback where the regional one is missing or fails.
- Rule, the same for every region (0022 still holds: no region is favoured): a regional
  service qualifies only if it is official, at least as recent, and finer in scale or richer
  in attributes than the national one; a clip of a national layer does not qualify. Legal
  status (bodies at risk, public water domain) comes from the competent authority. Every
  region is surveyed before any regional layer is shown; each tap names the source, scale
  and date that answered.
- Rejected: national only (throws away better local data); regional only where the field
  tests are (unequal quality).
