# 0035 — Rules narrowed to listed bodies and volume ranges; drilling needs the mining authority

- Date: 2026-11-26 · Status: accepted · MASTER_PLAN §4.5, decision 0034
- Decision: a rule's `where` may list groundwater bodies (as MITECO's layer writes them; a
  listed code also covers its `_NN` splits); any rule may apply only above or up to a volume
  (`applies_above_m3`, `applies_up_to_m3`), so concession-only zoning leaves a 7,000 m³/year
  well alone; `no_new_concessions` gains `when: always` for bodies or basins closed outright;
  distance cases gain a strict volume bound and an at-most flow bound, as the texts word them.
- A region whose water law adds nothing to a well is "loaded" with no rules; the knowledge
  file says what each law does. Every well needs the regional mining authority's approval of
  its project (RD 863/1985 arts. 108–109), a national rule, not a "not evaluated" permit.
- Rejected: a new rule kind per plan (the eight kinds cover every text read); unknown pump
  flow as the loosest case (it never fits a flow-bounded case).
