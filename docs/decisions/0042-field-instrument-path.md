# 0042 — No field instrument: contracted surveys when a spot needs one

- Date: 2026-11-27 · Status: accepted · Owner ruling · Supersedes 0008; BRIEF §5
- Context: session 16's live survey (`docs/knowledge/field-instruments.md`) recommended a
  100 m water-level meter (about €560-960), then a 4-electrode OhmPi v2024 meter for
  vertical soundings (about €1,100), about €1,700-2,000 in all.
- Decision (owner): nothing is bought or built. When a candidate spot justifies it, the
  owner hires a survey (a vertical sounding costs a few hundred euros per point, a
  tomography line thousands; drilling €25-150/m). Water levels the owner gets by any means
  are typed into the well registry by hand (session 17).
- Rejected: the recommended meter and sounding; the dipper alone; a 16-electrode OhmPi for
  tomography; HVSR, VLF, RTK and loggers (reasons in the knowledge file).
- Kept: observation kinds and hard rule 3 (MASTER_PLAN §4.8), unchanged.
