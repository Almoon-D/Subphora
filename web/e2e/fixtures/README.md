# Browser-test fixtures

What the API answers, produced on 2026-11-25 by `create_app` over the recorded answers in
`tests/fixtures/parcels/` and `tests/fixtures/search/`, or over the synthetic services of
`tests/national_support.py` (no network). Every place is public. The national map, its
explanation and the detailed maps come from the synthetic Spain and are regenerated by
`uv run python scripts/e2e_fixtures.py` (last on 2026-11-26).

| File | Request |
|---|---|
| `area-santiago.json` | `GET /api/aoi/cadastral/7279701NH3477G`: the parcel of the Praza do Obradoiro, Santiago de Compostela |
| `area-sigpac-campos.json` | `GET /api/aoi/sigpac/34:48:0:0:13:12:1`: an enclosure in Tierra de Campos |
| `search-daimiel.json` | `GET /api/search?q=Daimiel`: CartoCiudad's towns, municipality and streets |
| `area-daimiel.json` | `GET /api/aoi/place?type=Municipio&id=13039`: the municipality of Daimiel |
| `area-point-daimiel.json` | `GET /api/aoi/point?crs=OGC:CRS84&x=-3.6115&y=39.0675&origin=coordinates` |
| `map.json` | `GET /api/map` with the default configuration |
| `national.json` | `GET /api/national` after a national build of two synthetic boxes, in Madrid and around Daimiel, with productive boreholes along their valleys (`tests/national_support.py`) |
| `explain-madrid.json` | `GET /api/national/explain?lon=-3.70&lat=40.42` over that build: a validated domain |
| `parcel-queued.json` | `POST /api/parcel` with `area-point-daimiel.json`, before its job ran |
| `parcel-daimiel.json` | `GET /api/parcel/{id}` once that job is done, on the synthetic services with synthetic piezometric levels nearby: a validated domain, each candidate with its depth and cost |
| `parcel-campos.json` | the same for `area-sigpac-campos.json`, outside the national build and far from every piezometer: not validated, depth unknown |
| `explain-parcel-daimiel.json` | `GET /api/parcel/{id}/explain` at the Daimiel map's first candidate, after one synthetic productive well was logged 40 m east of it with one made-up reading at rest (its `updated` value and its `depth`, public and with the well) |
| `section-daimiel.json` | `POST /api/section` along a 1.2 km line running east through the Daimiel point, on the synthetic services: three units, a projected contact, the fault, a borehole and the water table |
| `legal-daimiel.json` | `GET /api/legal` at the Daimiel point's centre (region ES-CM) on the synthetic services: the synthetic body in poor status, Tajo plan and regional law not loaded, distances not measured |

The layer "updated with your wells" of the two detailed maps, the `updated` value and the
`depth` of the tap, and the empty `own_levels` of every depth were merged by hand from
regenerations of 2026-11-27; the rest of these files is from
the regeneration of 2026-11-26 (a full one also changes the legal answers, which the
browser tests of the legal panel read as they are).

`tests/unit/test_map_catalogue.py` checks that they still match the API's models (and
`map.json` its answer); regenerate them when it fails.
